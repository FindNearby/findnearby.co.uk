Fully free key searches for drinking water, accessibility, family and health needs

Find Nearby helps with lots of everyday searches, not only accessibility. Drinking water and key accessibility, family and health searches are fully free with no ads because they can matter most when people are out and about.

Key searches fully free with no ads

  • Drinking water: 2,782 mapped UK and Ireland locations
  • Accessible toilets: 5,109 mapped UK and Ireland locations
  • Accessible parking: 29,115 mapped UK and Ireland locations
  • Baby-changing toilets: 1,718 mapped UK and Ireland locations
  • Parent & child parking: 2,013 mapped UK and Ireland locations
  • Defibrillators: 23,207 mapped UK and Ireland locations
  • Hospitals: 2,071 mapped UK and Ireland locations
  • GPs / doctors: 11,415 mapped UK and Ireland locations

Why it matters

For many people, knowing where the nearest accessible toilet, accessible parking space, GP, hospital or baby-changing toilet is can shape whether a journey feels manageable.

Find Nearby is designed around real-life “where’s the nearest...?” moments. The app still includes toilets, parking, benches, playparks, drinking water, cash machines, chemists, bus stops and many more categories; these key searches are simply kept fully free with no ads because they can be especially important.
