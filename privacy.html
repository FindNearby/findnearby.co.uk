Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 May 2026

Scope: This Privacy Policy applies worldwide to your use of the Find Nearby app on Android and iPhone, and to the Find Nearby website where relevant.

1. Who We Are

Find Nearby is operated by Find Nearby Ltd (“Find Nearby”, “we”, “us”, “our”).

Company name: Find Nearby Ltd
Address: Clyde Offices (2nd Floor), 48 West George Street, Glasgow, Scotland, G2 1BP
Email: info@findnearby.co.uk

If you have any privacy questions or requests, please contact us at info@findnearby.co.uk.

2. Our Privacy Approach

We designed Find Nearby to use as little personal data as possible.

In summary:

  • you do not need to create an account with your name or email to use the app
  • we do not store your precise location history on our own servers
  • you can search by place name without granting location permission
  • we use advertising, analytics, diagnostic, referral, credits, and subscription tools to operate and improve the app
  • if you purchase Find Nearby Pro, payment is handled by the relevant app store provider rather than directly by us

3. What Information We Use

a) Location data

If you allow location access, the app uses your device location to:

  • show relevant places near you
  • calculate distances
  • support routing, directions, and related map features

We do not store your precise location history on our own servers.

You can turn location permission off at any time in your device settings. If you do, you can still search by place name instead.

b) Search, map, and routing data

If you type a place name, address, or search query into the app, that information may be sent to our map and location providers so they can return search results, autocomplete suggestions, maps, routes, or Street View imagery where available.

c) Advertising data

We currently use Google AdMob to show ads. We may also use Unity Ads as a fallback or alternative advertising provider.

On Android:

  • if you consent, ads may be personalised
  • if you do not consent, we request non-personalised ads

On iPhone:

  • we currently request non-personalised ads
  • we do not currently request App Tracking Transparency permission for advertising in the app

Even when ads are non-personalised, advertising partners may still process limited technical data such as device or app identifiers, IP-derived region, and ad interaction data for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Where required, we use Google’s consent/privacy messaging tools to show privacy and advertising choices.

You can review or change relevant ad/privacy choices inside the app in Settings, where available.

d) Analytics and measurement

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and to improve performance, stability, and feature decisions.

This may include:

  • app events such as first open, screen views, and feature usage
  • device and app information such as app version, device model, and operating system
  • approximate region or country
  • app instance or installation identifiers

You can switch analytics off inside the app in Settings.

e) Crash reports and diagnostics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to help us understand and fix app crashes, errors, and stability issues.

Crash and diagnostic information may include:

  • device model
  • operating system and version
  • app version
  • crash logs
  • basic technical diagnostics
  • installation or app instance identifiers used by Firebase services

Crash reports help us make the app more reliable and identify issues affecting real users.

f) Anonymous app identifier, credits, and referrals

To support app features such as credits, referrals, and anti-abuse checks, the app may create an anonymous identifier using Firebase services.

This does not require your name or email address.

If you use referral features, we may process limited data such as:

  • anonymous app or user IDs
  • referral codes
  • reward or credit events
  • timestamps
  • basic anti-fraud signals
  • install attribution data where supported

If you never use the referral feature, we do not use referral codes for you.

g) Pro subscriptions and purchases

If you purchase Find Nearby Pro:

  • the purchase is processed by the relevant app store provider, such as Google Play or Apple
  • we do not receive your full payment card number or bank details
  • we may receive subscription status, entitlement, renewal, cancellation, restore, and transaction-related information needed to unlock or restore Pro features

We use Qonversion to help manage subscriptions, entitlements, purchase status, and restore flows.

h) Website contact data

If you email us or contact us through a link on the website, we will use your email address and message content to respond to your enquiry and keep appropriate support records.

i) Technical and diagnostic data

Like most apps and websites, we and our service providers may process limited technical data needed to operate and secure the app and website, such as:

  • device model
  • operating system and version
  • app version
  • browser type where relevant
  • language or locale
  • country or region
  • basic diagnostics
  • identifiers used by our SDKs and services

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide nearby search and map features
  • provide routing, directions, Street View, and navigation-related functionality
  • deliver ads and measure ad performance
  • run the credits and referral systems
  • enable Pro subscriptions and restore purchases
  • improve app stability, performance, and usability
  • prevent abuse, fraud, and misuse
  • respond to support or privacy enquiries
  • comply with legal obligations

5. Legal Bases for Processing (UK / EEA Users)

Where UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, we generally rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract: to provide the features you ask us to provide, such as nearby search, maps, routing, and Pro access
  • Consent: where required, for personalised advertising and certain privacy choices
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the app secure, prevent abuse, understand app performance, run non-personalised ads, operate referrals and credits, respond to enquiries, and improve the service
  • Legal obligation: where we must retain or disclose certain information by law

You can withdraw consent at any time for processing that depends on consent.

Depending on the feature you use, the app may use third-party providers such as:

  • Google AdMob, for ads and ad measurement
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) or related Google privacy messaging tools, for consent and privacy choices where required
  • Google Analytics for Firebase, for analytics and measurement
  • Firebase Authentication, for anonymous app or user identification where needed
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions, for app logic such as referrals, credits, and related records

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data for money.

We may share limited data with third parties only where needed to:

  • run the app
  • deliver ads
  • measure ad or app performance
  • operate subscriptions
  • process referral and credits logic
  • prevent fraud or abuse
  • respond to support or privacy enquiries
  • comply with the law

For some U.S. state privacy laws, personalised advertising may be treated as sharing for targeted advertising purposes. Where required, we provide ways to change those choices inside the app.

8. Data Retention

We keep data only for as long as needed for the purposes described above.

In general:

  • precise location: used in real time and not stored by us as a location history
  • app search and location requests: retained by relevant providers according to their own policies
  • analytics data: retained according to our analytics settings and provider retention rules
  • crash and diagnostic data: retained according to Firebase Crashlytics settings and provider retention rules
  • ad-related data: retained by ad partners according to their policies
  • referral and anti-abuse records: retained only for as long as reasonably needed to operate the programme, investigate abuse, and audit rewards
  • subscription and entitlement data: retained as long as needed to manage Pro access, restore purchases, comply with legal or accounting obligations, and resolve support issues
  • support emails: retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond to your enquiry and maintain appropriate business records

Where applicable to our own systems, referral and anti-abuse records may be retained for up to 24 months, then deleted or irreversibly aggregated.

When we no longer need data, we delete it or keep only aggregated or de-identified information where appropriate.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • access your data
  • correct inaccurate data
  • request deletion
  • object to certain processing
  • restrict certain processing
  • request portability where applicable
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

You can also:

  • turn location permission off in device settings
  • change ad and privacy choices in the app, where available
  • turn analytics off in the app
  • manage or cancel subscriptions through your app store account

To make a privacy request, contact: info@findnearby.co.uk

If we use an anonymous app or user identifier and you want us to investigate, delete, or stop using associated records where reasonably possible, email us with as much detail as you can and we will do our best to help.

If you are in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): https://ico.org.uk

Where required, we rely on appropriate legal safeguards for these transfers.

13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect data, including secure transmission where appropriate and limiting access to data where possible.

No system is completely secure, but we work to reduce risk and protect your information.
