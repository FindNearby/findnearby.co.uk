Find Nearby website v9 final update

Changes in this version:
- Updated homepage, features, coverage and FAQ wording to explain that Find Nearby has 1.16 million+ mapped amenities across the UK and Ireland, while also supporting worldwide searches using public map data where available.
- Clarified that searches outside the UK and Ireland may be slower and less complete.
- Changed the top navigation Download button to Download options, pointing to /#download instead of directly opening Google Play. This avoids sending desktop/Mac visitors straight to the Play Store and lets them choose iPhone or Android.
- Kept app-ads.txt and get/index.html from the previous GitHub/referral setup.
- Kept Netlify redirects, sitemap, old #privacy/#terms compatibility, screenshots, icons, privacy page, terms page and Clyde Offices address.
